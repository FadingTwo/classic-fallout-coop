# Roadmap

Where co-op is headed. These are plans, not promises, and there are no
dates: each step starts when the one before it works well.

## 1. Fallout 1 (available now)

Two-player co-op for the whole game: LAN, online and one PC. Since 1.7
also a marker to point things out (G), giving each other items, and an
optional knockout instead of game over, since 1.10 controller and
Steam Deck support, since 1.11 both players talk to people. More
[ideas](https://github.com/FadingTwo/classic-fallout-coop/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aenhancement)
are waiting; add a 👍 to the ones you want.

## 2. Fallout 2 (now)

Co-op for Fallout 2, built on
[Fallout 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/alexbatalov/fallout2-ce),
in the same package: the launcher finds Fallout 1 and Fallout 2 and lets
you pick. All of Fallout 1 co-op carried over (network, server, menus,
controller, conversations, giving items, knockout, bringing your own
character, trading, up to four players). Out of beta since 1.14: it passes
the same tests as Fallout 1, from every map to the ending.

## 3. Up to four players, and a harder mode (new)

Room for four players in Fallout 1 and Fallout 2, each with their own
view, and an optional harder setting (HARDER when hosting) where enemies
hold out longer the more players there are. New in 1.13: please report
what breaks.

## 4. Big Fallout 2 mods

Total conversions made by other teams on the Fallout 2 engine:

- **Fallout: Sonora**: playable since 1.17 (the launcher downloads it)
- **Fallout: Nevada**: playable since 1.18 (installed with its makers'
  installer; the launcher finds it)
- **Fallout 1.5: Resurrection**: playable since 1.19 (the launcher
  downloads it, or finds its installer's copy)
- **Olympus 2207**: playable since 1.20 (installed with its makers'
  installer; the launcher finds it)

Others rely more on sfall, an extension of the original Fallout 2 engine
that Fallout 2 CE only partly supports, so they come one at a time. You
will still need the mod itself from its
makers; nothing of theirs is included here. This project is not affiliated
with them.
